✧ chapter zero / a little hello ✧

every little heart
needs a quiet place.

Meet Miso: a small orange-and-cream tabby with curious paws, a soft little meow, and one very important job — keeping you company. She likes glowing flowers, midnight walks, and sitting beside you when words feel too heavy.

One night, she followed a firefly into five secret worlds. Now she saves kind notes in flowers for anyone who needs them. You can wander, stop, pet her, or simply stay. You never have to prove yourself to Miso.

Only used here to make Miso's notes feel personal. Not saved or shared.
