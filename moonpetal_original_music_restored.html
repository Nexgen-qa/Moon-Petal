A Message from the Founder

♡ Inspired by my Anastasia

Miso was inspired by my Anastasia, my orange-and-white tabby who kept me company through turbulent times. I created Moonpetal with one little wish: everyone deserves a little Anastasia of their own.

☾ this little garden is better with you in it

Meet Miso, a curious orange-and-cream tabby who loves moonlit walks, glowing flowers, and sitting beside someone who needs a friend.

She doesn't ask you to be brave, busy, or anything other than yourself. She'll just wander with you through five little worlds, leaving kind words among the flowers.
